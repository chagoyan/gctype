    Class(es):

    Creates "Typing Test Results <number>" in each checked class, filed under "Pending" like other tool-created assignments. Only assignments this tool creates can later be graded by it — Classroom refuses to let one app patch a grade on coursework another app (or a person, by hand) created.

    Grade

    Upload a Typing.com "Test Activity Details" CSV export. Grading rule: a "3 Minute Typing Test" dated inside your chosen window, turned in = full points; taken but not turned in = 80%; no qualifying test once the window closes = 0. Other test lengths (1 Minute, 5 Minute, 1 Page) are ignored. The CSV is parsed entirely in your browser — it is never uploaded anywhere.

    Number must match the "Typing Test Results <number>" assignment you created above for this checkpoint.
